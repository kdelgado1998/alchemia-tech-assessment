What the list is telling us

3 findings from the ranked data
Chain concentration

20 of 25 accounts belong to three retail chains — HY-VEE, Sam's Club, and Costco. A handful of corporate-level conversations could unlock most of this list, rather than 25 separate pitches.

Geographic clustering

Accounts concentrate in four metro areas: Des Moines / West Des Moines, Iowa City / Coralville, Cedar Rapids, and the Quad Cities. A regional sales route would beat working the list top-to-bottom.

One recency outlier

Lot-A-Spirits ranks #19 on historical value but hasn't sold gin since Oct 2022 — 3.5 years stale versus every other account, most active as recently as this spring. Worth confirming it's still an operating account before outreach.
